Pen and Paper Pictionary

How to play

  1. Split into 2 to 4 teams. One phone runs the words and the clock; draw on paper.
  2. On your team's turn, pick a drawer. Everyone else looks away while the drawer taps to reveal the secret word.
  3. The drawer may skip the word once per turn, then taps Start the clock and draws. No letters, numbers or talking.
  4. Teammates shout guesses. If they get it before time runs out, tap Guessed it. When time is up, the group says whether the team got it.
  5. A correct guess scores one point. Everyone gets the same number of turns, and the highest score wins.
  1. Everyone plays on their own phone, 2 to 8 players. You take turns drawing, in a random order that is the same on every phone.
  2. When it is your turn, tap Start drawing. You see the secret word and the clock starts. Draw it on the canvas: no letters or numbers.
  3. Everyone else watches your drawing live and types guesses. You may skip the word once per turn.
  4. The first correct guess ends the turn: 2 points for the guesser and 1 for the drawer. If time runs out, nobody scores.
  5. Everyone draws the chosen number of times. The highest score wins.

Pause stops the clock. The menu pauses it too; tap Resume when you are ready.
